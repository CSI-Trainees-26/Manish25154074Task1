Hati said the moon and the sun aren't rivals, I still believe in that.

Bond of Eclipse

Releases the sunpower forward in a 30m fan-shaped range to tag the first undowned enemy hit for 10s and creates a link with them (visible in 40m to only the skill user and their tagged enemy). If the user reduces the tagged enemy's HP to 30 or below, the enemy is knocked down instantly. If the enemy is knocked down or eliminated while tagged, moonpower will reset the skill and restore the user's HP by 10/s for 3s in total. Cooldown: 45s.
